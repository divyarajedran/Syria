1 · The logo

A heart drawn from two interlocking ribbons — care passing through a gate. The ribbon colour runs from cyan (open, welcoming) to indigo (trusted, clinical). The wordmark repeats the same gradient left to right.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the height of the “C” in the wordmark on every side.

Stacked logo ≥ 72 px tall · horizontal ≥ 28 px tall · mark alone ≥ 20 px · below that use the favicon/app-icon tile.

Which one when

Splash & marketing → stacked.
App bar / web nav / email → horizontal.
Launcher, avatars → app icon / avatar.
Dark screens → reverse.
One-colour print → mono.

Don’t

Stretch or squash

3 · Typography

Rounded, friendly letterforms echo the wordmark. Arabic and Kurdish (Sorani) screens use Noto Sans Arabic; always lay out right-to-left.
